about

I design systems that are precise by intent, not by accident — from sketch to finished part.

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at UCLA with hands-on experience across the full design-to-manufacture pipeline: FEA in ANSYS, GD&T drawings, CNC and lathe machining, composite layups, and multi-part CAD assemblies in Fusion 360, SolidWorks, and Onshape.

Outside the machine shop I'm involved in ASME robotics, UCLA Creative Labs as a project lead, Theta Tau, X1 Robotics, and Pilipinos in Engineering.
